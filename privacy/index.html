Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The Relational Way runs Danny Cohen's courses, coaching and workshops in connected communication. The business is operated by Tikshoret Keshuva Ltd. (תקשורת קשובה בע"מ), Israel. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • What you give us in forms: your name, email and the answers you write when you apply for a course, sign up for a session or join our newsletter.
  • Payment details: payments go through PayPal. We see the amount and your name and email, never your card details.
  • Course participation: if you join a course, your account on our learning platform (Mighty Networks) and your attendance on Zoom.
  • Basic site analytics: our site uses the Meta (Facebook) pixel to understand which pages and adverts are useful.

How we use it

  • To run the courses and sessions you sign up for, and to send you the details you need.
  • To send our newsletter, if you subscribed. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To keep the records the law requires, such as receipts.

We never sell your information.

Who helps us

We use a few trusted services to do this work: Kit (email), Zapier (connecting our forms), PayPal (payments), Mighty Networks (course platform), Zoom (live sessions), Google Workspace (records) and our accountants. Each only gets what it needs for its part.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from any email at any time. You can also ask us to show you, correct or delete what we hold about you. Just email mike@artofcommunication.life.
