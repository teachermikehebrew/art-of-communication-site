Stop Feeling Stuck in Your Head. Start Living in Deep Connection.

You long for authentic connection but find yourself overthinking, avoiding conflict, or repeating the same painful patterns. You're not broken — you just need the right tools and community to help you communicate from your heart instead of your head.

The worst part? You know how you want to show up, but in charged moments, you lose access to that grounded, compassionate version of yourself.

HI, I'M DANNY

I know how painful it can be to feel cut off — from yourself, from others, and from the kind of life you long to live. The anxiety, self-criticism, and patterns that leave you feeling alone or distant in relationships aren't your fault.

For the past 15 years, I've helped thousands of people — parents, professionals, and seekers — move past these struggles. My background includes trainings in Nonviolent Communication, many kinds of psychotherapy, and over a year of silent meditation retreats.

I believe life is better when we're in flowing, authentic connection with ourselves and each other. I'm here to guide you with both compassion and practical tools, so you can speak with clarity, build trust, and experience the intimacy, confidence, and aliveness you've been missing.
