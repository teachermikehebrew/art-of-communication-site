Torah is about relationship.

Bein adam la-chavero relations run through your everyday interactions, shape the contours of your collaboration with colleagues, board members, funders, the family at the graveside, the whole gamut of what community members bring to you. Most of us were taught some relational and communication skills, but they often fall short of empowering you to navigate those situations towards really satisfying outcomes, and to do so while maintaining your own integrity and standing for what you believe without polarizing (is that even possible?? yes).

Three places the gap shows up:

Connection

A conversation matters and you can feel yourself managing it instead of being in it — hyper vigilant about choosing words, tracking how you're coming across, a half-step back from the person in front of you, not knowing how to really reach the other, or to walk the line of being both honest and compassionate.

Conflict

Conflict in the community pulls you in. You absorb it or you armor against it, you leave depleted either way, and the underlying issue rarely resolves in a real way. You might believe that compromise is the best you can hope for.

Capacity

You hold everyone's grief and everyone's simcha, often on the same day, and there's nowhere obvious for what that does to you. It accumulates.

None of that means something's wrong with you or that it must be this way. The bugs are part of the standard relational operating system, but it’s not the only one we have available. Navigating these contexts calls for skills, and most rabbis never got the same level of thorough, nuanced training that they did for learning and teaching text, theology, or leading prayer. That's what these eleven weeks are for.

Who is this for?

Rabbis and Jewish spiritual leaders in every kind of setting. Pulpit rabbis with communities large and small. Educators and heads of school. Hillel and campus professionals. Chaplains — hospital, hospice, military, prison. Rabbis running organizations, movements, foundations. Rabbis doing community organizing. Independent and entrepreneurial rabbis with no institution behind them. Rabbis between roles. Rabbis whose work is almost all one-to-one, and rabbis who rarely stand before a congregation.

Much rabbinic programming assumes a shul and a bimah. This path and these skills will give you so much support for that context. And if your rabbinate doesn't look like that, you belong here just as much. Being present with people, working with conflict, carrying others' pain without losing yourself, finding the path that makes ואהבת לרעך כמוך an everyday possibility rather than a hopeful but all too distant aspiration — that's the job in every setting where rabbis work.

The Torah underneath it

Torah treats the space between people as an avodah — a place of practice, healing, and revelation. The Shechina rests between two who sit together with words of Torah between them (Pirkei Avot 3:2). "Love your fellow as yourself," said R' Akiva, is the klal gadol of the whole Torah.

The material puts that beside contemporary teachers of relational practice: Marshall Rosenberg's Nonviolent Communication, Thomas Hübl on attunement, Robert Gonzales on needs as "the doorway to spirit." 

The focus is on practical training, but I’m excited about sharing the Torah that inspires me and informs the book I’m working on (tentatively titled, Alive Together: Cultivating a Path of Sacred Relationality)

We'll Learn Together

1 Foundations of Relational Dynamics

Build the foundation you can learn to rest in. Learn the nervous system map of Polyvagal Theory to understand how your communication elicits either threat responses or collaborative states in the nervous system, learn to navigate toward the "green zone" where people are naturally cooperative, and discover the key skill that transforms dialogue from competition into collaboration.

2 Consciousness of Domination or Collaboration

Replace the right/wrong, power-over thinking with needs-based relating. Discover a different understanding of what needs are. Like Neo seeing the Matrix code, you'll learn to see the life-needs that animate every interaction, opening pathways to connection, trust, safety, and mutually satisfying outcomes.

3 Emotional Intelligence 101

Discover why we have feelings and what to do with those "difficult" emotions that complicate relationships. Learn to access the embodied intelligence that feelings express and welcome them as gateways to connection and more congruent life choices.

4 Listening Magic

Master the "superpower" of attuned empathy. Learn the foundational dance steps that turn relating into an art — many find this to be the master key that unlocks relationships. Move beyond techniques to create genuine connection that doesn't depend on agreement. Learn a deeper, more experientially resonant approach than listening validation techniques like, “I hear you saying…” “That’s legitimate…”

5 Beyond Right and Wrong - Creating Shared Reality

Stop having endless arguments about who's right. Learn to create shared reality and talk through problems without triggering defensiveness. Discover the potent alternative to praise and compliments that fuels intrinsic motivation and richer connection.

6 "I Have a Dream" - How to Help People Enjoy Caring for Your Needs

Transform how you make requests. Instead of people feeling burdened or giving in from obligation, learn the art of requests that invite true giving, create more choice and agency, and generate aliveness in relationships.

7 Presence & Internal Alignment

Access your magnetic power through presence. Learn to connect coherently with yourself, move beyond people-pleasing and "shoulds," and access more inner freedom, choice, and self-compassion.

8 Effective Honesty - Don't Be Nice, Be Real

Speak truth with care. Learn to be skillfully real without "rocking the boat" or reinforcing that honesty comes at a cost. Leverage the power of human resonance to be both authentic AND relational.

9 Working Empathically with Grief

Learn to meet grief not as a problem to be solved, but as a sacred process to be honored. In a culture that often demands we "fix" or move past pain, discover how to slow down and offer high-quality, non-judgmental presence. Learn somatic and affective skills to stay anchored and regulated while navigating deep sorrow, turning moments of profound loss into opportunities for deep, respectful connection and healing.

10 Full Dialogue - Integration

Put all the dance steps together. Navigate the back-and-forth of real conversations with skill, even in charged contexts. Train in the art of collaborative dialogue to be more skillful and effective in your most important relationships.

FAQs

I'm not a congregational rabbi. Is this for me?

Yes — see "Who this is for." The cohort is built for rabbis across all settings, not the pulpit alone.

How much time does this actually require each week?

About two hours a week: video modules, short readings, reflections, and ideally a practice conversation with a cohort partner. Danny has also built an AI coach to offer you ample opportunity to practice and get personalized, precise feedback relevant to you and your growing edge.

Is this therapy or a support group?

No. It's a skills cohort. It asks for some existing psychological steadiness and a wish to widen your relational range.

Will it engage Torah and text directly?

Yes, there will be some Torah, however the emphasis is not on text or theology but on the practical path and practice that opens the way for deep tikkun and transformation.

What if I can't make every live session?

Consistent presence at live sessions is really valuable for bringing the learning alive and contributing to the learning field. Session times are listed above. Please plan around these times for the best experience. Please be in touch if you have a conflict with one of the session times at mike@artofcommunication.life

Can this really work over Zoom?

Absolutely. Participants consistently report deep connection and transformation in our online format. As one participant shared: "Learning and using NVC facilitated deepening relationships in our whole group, so every week we dropped back into a supportive, loving and curious community."

Will Danny be delivering the live sessions?

Yes Danny will be leading the live sessions accompanied by his team of highly skilled coaches.

What happens if the course doesn't reach critical mass?

The course proceeds contingent upon reaching sufficient enrollment. If we don't have enough participants, we'll notify you immediately and provide alternative options or full refunds.
